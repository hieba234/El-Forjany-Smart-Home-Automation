FAQ

Questions clients ask before booking.

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. The first consultation is free so the installer can understand the place and recommend the right setup.

Can you install devices I already bought?

Yes, if the devices are compatible and safe to install. Compatibility is checked during the visit.

Does it work with Alexa or Google Home?

Yes. The setup can be planned around Alexa, Google Home, or another compatible ecosystem.

Do I need strong Wi-Fi?

Smart homes need stable Wi-Fi. If the signal is weak, the visit includes network recommendations.

Do you work in apartments and villas?

Yes. The service can work for apartments, villas, offices, and shops.

Will I get exact pricing online?

The website can show starting prices, but the final quote should come after the free visit.
